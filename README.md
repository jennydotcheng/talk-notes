# Talk Notes

Record a conversation, transcribe it on your own device, label who said what, and hand the transcript to Claude for notes.

Open it at https://jennydotcheng.github.io/talk-notes/

- Works in Safari and Chrome on Mac and iPhone.
- After a one-time download of the speech models (about 125 MB per device), recording and transcription work with no internet.
- Audio is turned into text on the device and is not kept or uploaded. Transcripts are stored only in that browser.
