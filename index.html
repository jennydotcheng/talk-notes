One-time setup on this device

Talk Notes transcribes on this device, so it needs to download its speech models once (about 125 MB, best on Wi-Fi). After that it works with no internet, and your recordings never leave this device.

On iPhone: first tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen", and open Talk Notes from the new icon before you set up. That keeps the download from being cleared.
